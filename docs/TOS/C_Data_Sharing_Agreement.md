# Data Sharing Agreement

## MSP Platform Suite

**Effective Date**: 20 April 2026

# 1. Purpose

This Agreement governs the terms under which the Partner provides
geographical data and spatial datasets to Breda University of Applied
Sciences (hereinafter referred to as BUas) for use within the MSP
Platform Suite. It is a standalone agreement that operates under the
principles of the MSP Platform Suite Community Charter & General
Agreement.

This Agreement is used when a Partner — typically a governmental body,
research institution, or project collaborator — contributes its own data
to the custodian for curation and integration into the platform, often
in the context of a funded project or a commissioned workshop.

# 3. Partner Representations and Warranties

By signing this Agreement, the Partner represents and warrants that:

- **Ownership or authorisation:** The Partner either owns the data or
  has obtained all necessary rights and authorisations to share it with
  BUas under the terms selected in this Agreement.

- **No conflicting obligations:** Sharing the data does not violate any
  third-party rights, breach confidentiality obligations, or contravene
  applicable laws.

- **Voluntary contribution:** The Partner provides the data voluntarily
  and of its own free will.

- **Third-party components:** If the data includes or is derived from
  third-party sources, the Partner discloses all upstream sources and
  confirms that upstream licence terms are compatible with the selected
  use option (Section 5).

# 4. What BUas May Do With the Data

Regardless of which use option is selected in Section 5, BUas has the
right to:

- Convert, reformat, and adapt the data to the technical requirements of
  the MSP Platform Suite and its software products

- Combine the data with other datasets for integration into the platform

- Clean, validate, simplify geometries, and optimise for visualisation
  and simulation performance

- Generate derived metadata and documentation

- Store the data in its backend infrastructure for the purposes
  authorised by the selected option in section 5

BUas will not sell the Partner’s raw data as a standalone product under
any option.

# 5. Use Options

For each dataset listed in Section 8, the Partner selects one of the
following options. This determines what BUas may do with that specific
dataset beyond the baseline processing described in Section 4.

## Option 1 — Platform Integration Only (Most Restrictive)

The data may be used only within the MSP Platform Suite for the
Partner’s own purposes:

- Integration into the Partner’s Private Instance deployment

- Internal demonstrations to the Partner’s authorised users only

- Storage in BUas’s backend for the Partner’s authorised access

**Not permitted:** public demonstrations, distribution to other partners
or third parties, use in publicly accessible products, or any commercial
use.

## Option 2 — Platform Integration + Public Demonstrations + Use within MSP Platform Suite

Everything in Option 1, plus:

- Public demonstrations of the software products of the MSP Platform
  Suite

- Display in publicly accessible sessions and workshops managed by BUas

- Use by BUas-managed training and educational activities

- Integration into 3D visualisation products (e.g. Immersive Ocean,
  Procedural Ocean, GeoViz, AugGIS)

- Use in VR/AR applications and immersive demonstrations

- Creation of 3D models, visualisations, and derived visual products for
  non-commercial purposes

**Not permitted:** redistribution of raw data to third parties,
standalone use outside the MSP Platform Suite.

## Option 3 — Unrestricted Use Within MSP Platform Suite(Most Permissive)

BUas may use the data for any purpose within the MSP Platform Suite,
including all uses described above plus:

- Commercial services using the platform

- Integration into future products and services in the platform family

- Long-term archival and future development

“Unrestricted” is limited to use within the MSP Platform Suite. BUas
will not sell Partner’s raw data as a standalone product. The Partner’s
ownership of the original data remains intact.

# 6. Attribution

BUas will attribute the Partner’s contributed data in the platform’s
data source documentation
(<https://community.mspchallenge.info/wiki/Data_sources>) and, where
practicable, in the platform’s user interface.

The Partner specifies the preferred attribution text in the dataset
table (Section 8).

# 7. Confidentiality

Private or confidential data should be clearly marked by contributing
partners as limited to Option 1 only (no public use) at the time of
contribution.

By selecting Option 2 or 3, the Partner confirms that the contributed
data is not confidential and may be disclosed publicly per the selected
option.

# 9. Data Quality and Fitness for Purpose

The Partner provides data “as is.” BUas accepts the data as provided and
assumes responsibility for determining whether it is suitable for use
within the MSP Platform Suite. BUas makes no representations to third
parties about the accuracy, completeness, or fitness of the Partner’s
data.

If BUas discovers issues with the data during curation (invalid
geometries, missing values, incompatible formats), BUas will make
reasonable efforts to address them or notify the Partner.

# 10. Withdrawal and Termination

## 10.1 Partner Withdrawal

The Partner may withdraw specific datasets from this Agreement at any
time by written notice to BUas. BUas will cease new uses of the
withdrawn data within reasonable time depending upon the availability of
human resources. Withdrawal does not affect data already published in
scientific literature, integrated into completed projects, or made
available to third parties prior to withdrawal (if Option 2 or 3 was
selected).

## 10.2 BUas Termination

BUas may cease using the Partner’s data at any time, for example if
upstream licence changes make continued use untenable or if better data
sources become available.

Any changes in data sources will be documented in:
https://community.mspchallenge.info/wiki/Data_sources

## 10.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination of this Agreement:

- BUas will cease new uses of the Partner’s data

- BUas may retain archival copies for record-keeping, legal compliance,
  and completed research outputs

- The Partner’s ownership of the original data is unaffected

# 11. Liability and Indemnification

The Partner indemnifies BUas against claims arising from the Partner’s
data infringing third-party rights, the Partner lacking authority to
share the data, or the data violating upstream licence terms.

BUas’ liability under this Agreement is limited as set out in the Terms
& Conditions.

# 12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Disputes
shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the Netherlands.

# 13. Relationship to Other Documents

This Agreement operates under and is subject to the MSP Platform Suite’s
Community Charter & General Agreement. The Terms & Conditions apply to
the Partner’s own use of the platform. In case of conflict, the Charter
prevails.
