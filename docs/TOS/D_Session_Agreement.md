using the MSP Platform Suite and its products. It is agreed between
Breda University of Applied Sciences (BUas), acting as custodian of the

This Agreement operates under and is subject to the MSP Platform Suite’s
Community Charter & General Agreement (Document A). All participants of
the workshop will be subject to the Terms & Conditions for Users of the
MSP Platform Suite (Document B). In case of conflict between this
Agreement and the Charter, the Charter prevails.

# 5. Participant-Created Data

Data created by participants during the session — such as spatial plans,
drawn geometries, or scenario outputs — may contain or be derived from
underlying platform data layers, including restricted or privately
licensed data. This section determines what happens to that created
data.

## 5.1 Data Sharing Option

The commissioning party selects one of the following options for
participant-created data from this session:

| ☐ **A** | No data is released. All participant-created data remains with BUas and is not shared with the commissioning party or participants. Data may be retained by BUas for archival, research, or platform improvement purposes. |
|:--:|----|
| ☐ **B** | Data is released to the commissioning party only. BUas provides the participant-created data to the commissioning party after the session. The commissioning party assumes responsibility for any onward distribution to participants or third parties, subject to the upstream licence terms of any underlying data used in the session. |
| ☐ **C** | Data may be released to participants upon request. Participants may request their created data from BUas after the session. BUas reviews all export requests and releases data only where it is satisfied that the release is consistent with the upstream licence terms of any underlying data and the provisions of this Agreement. |

## 5.2 Custodian Review

Regardless of the option selected above, BUas reviews all data exports
before release. BUas retains the right to refuse any data export that it
determines may include restricted or privately licensed data in a manner
inconsistent with the applicable upstream licence terms, regardless of
what this Agreement permits.

BUas may also refuse export where it identifies that participant
annotations have been used to systematically replicate, trace, or derive
the substance of underlying data layers — for example, by
comprehensively marking the locations of infrastructure or features to
reconstruct data that the participant would not otherwise be entitled to
access. Such conduct constitutes a breach of the data restrictions set
out in the Terms & Conditions (Document B, Section 3.2).

# 6. Participant Notification

Participants in the session are not required to sign this Agreement.
Instead, participants are informed of the applicable data terms at the
start of the session. This notification covers:

- What data sources are used in the session (public, restricted, or
  both)

- What will happen to data created by participants during the session

- That photos and visual media may be captured (with the right to
  object)

- That by participating in the session, participants accept the Terms &
  Conditions (Document B)

Participants who do not wish their created data to be shared may notify
BUas or the commissioning party during the session or after. If any
participant objects, BUas will not release that participant’s created
data from that moment onwards.

# 7. Photo and Media Consent

BUas or the commissioning party may capture photos, screenshots, screen
recordings, or videos during the session for purposes including
documentation, education, training, marketing, media, and promotional
use.

Participants are informed at the start of the session and may object to
being photographed or recorded. Visual media capturing restricted or
private data may not be shared publicly without written consent from the
data provider and the commissioning party, in accordance with Document
B, Section 3.3.

# 9. Responsibilities

## 9.1 BUas Responsibilities

- Organise and facilitate the session using the MSP Platform Suite

- Ensure that only data authorised for the session is used

- Review all data exports before release

- Inform participants of the session’s data terms at the start of the
  session

- Retain session data for the agreed retention period

## 9.2 Commissioning Party Responsibilities

- Provide timely input, data, and information necessary for session
  preparation

- Ensure that participants are appropriately authorised to attend

- If receiving exported data under Option B, comply with all upstream
  licence terms and assume responsibility for onward distribution

- Where the commissioning party also shares data with BUas under a Data
  Sharing Agreement (Document C), ensure that the selected DSA option
  permits the intended use in this session
